Roads tab

Roads that join themselves

Drag to lay a straight road, up to 32 tiles at a time. Drag across or into another road and you get a corner, a T junction or a crossroads. Open ends finish in a rounded dead end with sidewalk all the way round.

  • Two wide lanes, a curb, sidewalk, grass strip and concrete edge on both sides, modelled on Steventechno's Buildings Pack 2.0 roads and widened so cars have room.
  • Rounded curbs on every inside corner.
  • Curved roads: press on the open end of one road and drag to another. A smooth curve joins them, even when they're offset, at an angle, at different heights or doubling back as a U-turn.
  • Lots line up flush with the sidewalk.
  • Built for driving: no seams to snag a wheel (curves too), curbs that ramp up in the collision, and slabs thick enough that fast cars don't fall through. Every piece is frozen solid like a physgunned prop, so simfphys, LVS and Glide cars drive on them.
  • The preview turns orange where a tile would land on a lot, and those tiles are skipped.

Flooring tab → Sidewalks

Drawn sidewalks and front walks

Draw sidewalk anywhere on the grid: a front walk from the road to the door, a wide path to the garage, a plaza. Drawn sidewalks snap to the height of the road sidewalk they touch so they meet it flush, and they can cross the grass strip to join it. Pick a texture to lay them with, drag over them to retexture, or Shift-click to fill a whole connected walk.

Roads tab → Driveway

Driveways

Drag along the sidewalk in front of a lot to set how wide the driveway is. The curb, sidewalk and grass are cut out and a concrete ramp runs from the lot down to the road on a smooth curve, with rounded sides easing into the sidewalk. Raise the lot's foundation and the ramp follows.

Roads tab → Roads (no sidewalk)

Country roads

Roads without sidewalks are just the road surface, with lots allowed right up to the edge. Where one meets a road with sidewalks, the sidewalk curves smoothly down to nothing so the two meet at the same width. Pair them with the Dirt Road or Country Lane style for back roads and farm tracks.

Home Furnishings → Outdoor

Street furniture on any sidewalk

Streetlamps, lantern streetlamps, fire hydrants, litter and wheelie bins, mailboxes, park benches, planters, bollards, traffic cones, traffic barrels and parking signs go straight onto road sidewalks and drawn sidewalks, no lot needed. They save with the map like roads and fences.

  • Streetlamps start off. E turns them on: they warm up, the glass glows and they light the street around them. Admins can switch every streetlamp on at once.
  • Sit on a park bench with E.
  • Only whoever placed a piece, or an admin, can move or remove it.
Public Furnishings → Parking Lots

Parking lots

Put Parking spaces side by side and their lines overlap into one painted row. Turn the next row round and put it back to back. Lay the lot itself in Road Asphalt floor tiles so it meets the street without a seam. Add wheel stops, speed bumps, parking lines for angled bays, a parking sign and an EV charger.

Public Furnishings → Skyscrapers

Skyscrapers and office towers

With Steventechno's Buildings Pack 2.0 installed, its "No Lot" skyscrapers appear in Public Furnishings, one piece each. Pick one, turn it to face the street and click. They're placed, moved and removed like furniture, and stay drawn from any distance so the skyline is always there.

Build your own towers

The Walls paint picker has office-window facades (Blue, Dark, Bronze and Green glass, Lights On, Concrete and Brick with windows) that line up floor to floor, so a tall building needs no real windows. Reflective Glass in five tints mirrors the sky. Add Drop Ceilings, Brushed Steel and the full-height Glass wall, then raise Max floors to 16.

Public Furnishings → Clubs

Nightclubs

Everything for a night out, placed with the same hand as furniture.

  • Light-up dance floor tiles with nine patterns: Rainbow wave, Disco squares, Pink and blue beat, Ripples, Sparkle, Colour fade, Fire, Ice and Checkerboard flash. Put tiles side by side and the patterns flow across them as one floor, all on the same 124 BPM beat.
  • Bar counters, a bar corner and a lit back bar with glowing light strips. Bar stools snap three to a counter, facing it.
  • A light tower whose four moving heads sweep coloured light round the room, and a disco ball that turns and sparkles.
  • A DJ booth, VIP sofa, high table, PA speaker and velvet rope.
  • Staging: runway tiles and a rounded runway end that line up into a catwalk, a 2x2 stage, stage steps, a pole stage, a glowing go-go box and dance poles in chrome, gold, rose gold, brass or black. Runways, stages and steps all share one height, so they fit together any way.
  • E switches lights on and off and sits you on stools and the VIP sofa.
