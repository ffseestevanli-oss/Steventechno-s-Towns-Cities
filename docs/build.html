Build mode · press F3

Build it your way

Everything in Build mode is drawn on a 64-unit grid with your mouse: drag for walls and floors, click for doors and windows. A green ghost shows what fits, red shows what doesn't, and Ctrl+Z undoes every step.

Flooring tab

Lots and foundations

Every house starts with a lot. Drag on empty ground to draw one anywhere from 4x4 up to 64x64 tiles. While the Lots tool is picked, every lot shows as a bright green grid.

  • Resize by the edges: grab any side or corner of a lot (it lights up yellow) and drag it in or out. Anything that would be cut off shows in red first.
  • Foundation is optional. Pick a height from 8 to 96 units and a texture, then drag it onto the lot. Change the height and the walls, stairs and everything else on the lot move with it.
  • Foundation edges take any Wall paint texture, so you can face a foundation in brick or stone.
  • Shift-click fills the whole foundation with a texture in one go.
PAINT column → Floor tiles

Floor tiles, ceilings and up to 16 floors

Pick a texture and drag: one stroke lays new tiles on empty cells and retextures the ones already there. Shift-click fills a whole room up to its walls. Look up at an upper floor from below and the same drag paints the ceiling instead.

  • Each storey sits 128 units above the last. Switch floors with the arrows on the left or Page Up / Page Down.
  • Buildings go up to 8 floors, and admins can raise that to 16 in Settings.
  • Everything above the floor you're on hides while you build, so you can always see what you're doing.
  • Upper floors can overhang the floor below by up to 2 tiles, for balconies and bay rooms.
  • Road-matching floors: the roads' own asphalt, sidewalk concrete, cobblestone and dirt, so a forecourt or parking lot meets the street without a seam.
  • Coloured concrete in light grey, charcoal, sand, terracotta, red, sage, slate blue and mocha for patios and plazas.
  • Tiles merge into as few pieces as possible: a 16x16 floor is a single prop.
Walls tab

Walls drawn on the grid

Drag from a grid corner for a straight wall, or hold Shift and drag to outline a whole room. Right-drag deletes. Walls are double-sided: indoor faces start in white paint, outdoor faces in white siding, and you can paint each side separately.

  • Wall view (Tab) switches between walls up, cutaway (front walls lowered so you can see in) and walls down. Lowered walls are cut cleanly with a trim cap, never squashed.
  • Corners close neatly on their own, or tick Corner posts for white posts at every corner.
  • Pillars: square or round, placed on any grid corner and painted like walls. A Corner cap gives a single corner the white post look.
  • Split line: put a paint line across your outdoor walls and paint above and below it separately, for brick below and siding above.
  • Move walls: grab a wall and slide it sideways. Its doors, windows, paint and pictures go with it, and the walls joined to it stretch to keep the room closed.
Walls tab

Diagonal walls and half walls

Diagonal walls: drag at roughly 45 degrees and the wall snaps to an exact diagonal from corner to corner. Rooms closed by a diagonal count as indoors, and floor tiles split into triangles along it, so each half can have its own floor.

Half walls: turn on Half walls and new walls are drawn at the height you choose. Press on a wall and move the mouse up or down to set its height live, in 4-unit steps (hold Shift for half-unit steps). Right-click a wall to make it full height again. Perfect for kitchen bars, railings and room dividers.

Doors tab

Doors that open

Pick a design and click a wall. Every door is framed on both faces and opens with E in game, swinging away from whoever opens it. Double doors open together, and F flips the hinge side of a single door.

  • Wood, panel, glass, double and French doors built from House Pack wood in walnut, cherry or smoked grey, with brass knobs and real see-through glass.
  • Six-panel doors in grey, white and black, an arched panel door, and double six-panel doors.
  • Doors that ship with the addon: Oak shaker, Craftsman, Navy front door, Red door with glass, Farmhouse plank, double versions, and city doors in green, brown and metal.
  • Glass sliding doors with a dark aluminium frame and real see-through glass, one panel sliding past the other.
  • Archways, single and wide: open, framed passages.
  • Garage doors from 2 to 8 tiles wide (the mouse wheel sets the width): the sections roll up the track and back along the ceiling. Put a garage door button on the wall and E on it opens every garage door in the room.

With an empty hand, click a door or window to pick it up and put it somewhere else. The wall closes up behind it.

Windows tab

Windows at any of ten heights

Press on a wall, keep holding and move the mouse up or down: the window snaps between ten height levels, from as low to as high as it fits. Release to place it. A plain click puts it at the default height.

With a window in your hand, the mouse wheel turns it in the wall, 15° at a time, for diamond and angled windows.

  • Classic set: Window, Paned, Tall and High windows, Wide and Wide paned, and a 3-tile Picture window.
  • Framed set with casing and a sill: Divided, Cottage, Transom, Double-hung, Four-pane, Sidelight, Grid and Twin grid.
  • Glass wall: a full-height glass panel for lobbies and curtain walls.
Stairs tab

Stairs and porch steps

Pick Oak, Walnut, Carpet, Stone or Concrete, point at the cell for the bottom step and click. Stairs climb exactly one storey over two cells, at a step height players walk up normally. The floor above opens up over the stairwell by itself, and closes again with its old paint if you remove them.

Porch steps snap to the edge of a foundation and always match its height: one step for every 16 units. Raise the foundation and they rebuild themselves.

A wall beside the stairs trims them to its face, so stairs in a one-tile hallway fit exactly between the walls.

Paint treads and risers with Floor tiles and the sides with Wall paint.

Stairs tab → Elevators

Working elevators

Build a 2x2 elevator shaft in Brushed steel, Red brick, White, Charcoal, Concrete or Sand stucco. Set the top stop with [ and ], turn the doors, and click. Every floor it passes is cut open for the shaft, and the top gets a roof cap.

  • E on the shaft or its doors calls the car. The CALL button lights up while it comes.
  • E inside the car opens the floor buttons. Pick a floor and ride.
  • Doors slide open at each stop and stay open while someone stands in the doorway.
  • A display above every landing shows the car's floor and direction.
  • Everyone in the car rides it like a vehicle: you stay where you stood, facing the same way, with no bobbing, and step off on your floor.
Pools & Basement tab

Shallow pools and basements

Pools: drag a rectangle on bare ground of your lot, up to 16x16 tiles. Pick Wading or Shallow and white, blue, teal or stone tiles. Walk in and you wade, drawn lower in the water with splashing footsteps.

Basements: dig one room a storey down under the house, then click in the stairs that come up through floor 1. While the tool is open the room shows through the ground. Down there you walk, run and jump as usual, and other players see you. Fill in closes it again.

Roofs tab

Roofs in five shapes

Pick a shape, a pitch and a texture, then drag over the rooms. The roof sits right on top of the walls with a half-tile overhang and a flat soffit underneath.

  • Gable
  • Hip
  • Flat
  • Lean-to
  • Half hip
  • Shallow 14°
  • Low 21°
  • Medium 27°
  • Steep 37°
  • Lean-to and Half hip are cut flat at the back, for porches and extensions against a taller wall.
  • F turns a gable's ridge. Click an existing roof to give it the current shape, pitch and texture.
  • Roofs can overlap, so an L-shaped house is just two roofs.
  • Paint the top, the underside and the gable ends (inside and out) separately.
  • Hide roofs tucks every roof away while you work inside.
Walls tab → Fences

Fences and gates, anywhere

Drag a fence along the grid, or Shift-drag to fence a whole yard at once. Click any edge with the Gate tool to add a gate that opens with E, swinging away from you.

  • Picket
  • Privacy
  • Ranch
  • Iron
  • Fences go on empty ground, along roads and on your own lots.
  • On a lot they stand on the foundation or the current floor, so Page Up gives you balcony and roof-deck railings. They move with the foundation and go with the house if you bulldoze it.
  • Off a lot they follow the ground, stepping up and down slopes.
PAINT column

Paint everything

Two blue tabs at the front of the tab strip, Floor tiles and Wall paint, paint everything that isn't a roof or a road. Pick how much to cover (one tile, a dragged area, the whole room or the whole floor) and which side of a wall (the one you click, inside, outside or both). Alt or Ctrl-click is an eyedropper that picks up any texture you point at. Roofs and roads keep their own Paint buttons.

  • The addon's own paints, wallpapers, siding, brick, stucco, wood, tile, carpet, stone and shingles.
  • More than 220 textures from Steventechno's House Pack 3 and Buildings Pack 2.0 show up in the picker when those packs are installed, sorted into groups.
  • Swatch cards group the colour versions of one texture together, like furniture styles.
  • City finishes: office-window facades, reflective glass, drop ceilings and brushed steel (see Skyscrapers).
Everywhere

Handy tools

  • Eraser: one Remove tool for every tab. Turn it on and a click or drag removes whatever that tab would add.
  • Pipette: click any furniture, door, window or wall object you already placed and a copy, in the same style, lands in your hand with its tab opened.
  • Cursor icons: a sledgehammer, hand, paint brush or eyedropper next to the pointer shows what a click will do.
  • Interface sounds for placing, removing, picking up, turning, undo and switching floors, and a different one when something can't go there.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z undoes every drag, paint, placement and removal.
  • Half-size grid: Shift+G switches to a 32-unit snap grid for finer placement.
  • Camera: WASD or the screen edges slide, middle-drag orbits, right-drag pans, the wheel zooms, Q / E rotate 45° and H re-centres.
  • Bulldoze: click a lot to clear it and everything on it in one go (it asks first, and undo brings it back).
