Servers, saves and comfort

Build together, keep everything

Made for servers as much as single player: fair limits for players, live previews of what everyone is building, auto-save so nothing is lost, and level of detail so a whole town keeps running smoothly.

For servers

Fair build limits

Players who aren't admins get sensible limits out of the box, so one builder can't fill the whole map. Single player and admins never have limits.

  • Props are Furnish things: furniture, lights, fans, kitchen and bathroom pieces and wall decor. Walls, floors, doors, windows, stairs, roofs, fences and roads don't count.

See each other build

Live previews

While someone drags walls, floor tiles, a foundation or a lot, everyone else sees the same ghost in blue with the builder's name over it (orange for removals). It updates about ten times a second and disappears when they let go. Don't want to see them? sthc_mp_previews 0 hides them.

Top bar

Bulldoze

Click Bulldoze, then click a lot to remove it and everything built on it: house, furniture, fences and all. It asks first, and Ctrl+Z brings it all back. Players can only bulldoze their own lots, admins any.

Never lose a build

Auto-save

Lots, houses, furniture, roads, fences and street furniture save with the map. Admins get a Save button in the top bar with:

  • Auto-save on or off: saves every 5 minutes when something changed, and on shutdown or map change. The town loads again when the map starts.
  • Save now and Load last save.
  • Remove all, which asks first.
New

Distance level of detail

Big towns stay smooth. Each player's game decides, lot by lot, how much to draw based on how far away it is:

  • Near: everything.
  • Further: furniture, wall decor and lamps are hidden.
  • Far: the whole house becomes one simple, low-detail model in the right colours, with glass shown as dark blue-grey.

Nothing changes on the server, so doors, curtains, seats and lights are exactly as you left them when you come back. Turn it off or tune the distances in Settings → Performance.

Make it yours

An interface you can rearrange

  • Docked or floating: press Float to split the interface into windows you can drag anywhere, and Dock to put it back.
  • Your tab order: the pencil at the end of the tab strip lets you drag tabs into your own order or hide the ones you don't use.
  • Classic look: switch to the Garry's Mod spawn menu style (or your skin mod) in Settings.
  • Grid: show or hide it with G, and pick a half-size grid for Build and Furnish separately.
Under the hood

Solid like a frozen prop

Every built piece, from walls and floors to roofs, stairs, fences and roads, collides like a prop frozen with the physgun. Players, thrown props and vehicles all hit them properly, and nobody can knock your house over: physgun and gravity gun pickups are blocked, and anything another addon unfreezes freezes again within two seconds.
