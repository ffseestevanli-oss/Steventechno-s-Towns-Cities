Furnish mode · press F2

Make it a home

Furnish works like a Sims-style hand. Click an item to pick it up, press where it goes and drag to turn it, then let go. Pieces snap flush to walls, turn to face into the room, and small things land on tables and shelves.

How it works

The hand

  • Click an icon and the item is in your hand. A see-through ghost and an arrow on the floor show exactly where it goes and which way it faces.
  • Press and drag to turn it in 90° steps, or use , and .. Hold Alt to place freely at any angle.
  • Hold Shift as you put it down to keep it in your hand and place more.
  • With an empty hand, click a placed piece to lift it and move it. Del deletes it, right-click puts it back.
  • Furniture sits with its back flush on the wall and turns to face away from it. In a corner it keeps a facing that fits.
  • If a model faces the wrong way, F turns it while the arrow stays. Your PC remembers it for that model.

Things go on things

TVs, monitors, lamps, vases, plants, clocks and other small pieces stand on tables, desks, dressers, shelves, counters and cabinets. Move the table and everything on it comes along. Delete it and they drop to the floor.

Chairs snap to tables and you can sit on them

Hold a chair near a desk and it tucks in at the front. Near a dining table it takes the nearest free seat on any edge. Every chair, from the packs, HL2 or the addon's own, seats you with E.

Colours and finishes

Pick a style on the swatch card

Items with several styles have a swatch badge on their icon. Click one and a card opens with a live, turning preview and a round swatch for every colour, fabric or wood. Hover to preview, click to pick. [ and ] change the style of whatever's in your hand, and the last style you chose comes back next time.

This works for the furniture packs' skins and for every built-in piece.

Towns & Cities → Kitchen

Kitchens that build themselves

Counters side by side join into one run with one countertop. Turn one at the end of a run and it becomes a corner: a blind corner for an inside L, or a capped end with doors on both faces for an outside corner. Drop a stove, fridge, dishwasher or sink onto a counter and it swaps in.

  • E turns the stove's burners on (they glow red), runs the sink, and sits you on a bar stool.
  • Counters against a wall get a backsplash. Islands keep a seating overhang for stools.
  • Countertop patterns run on from piece to piece and round corners.
  • Sized to match Steventechno's Furniture Pack, so they sit nicely next to the pack's own kitchen pieces.

Towns & Cities → Lighting

Lights, fans and switches

Ceiling lights, pendants and fans mount under the ceiling. Sconces and light switches snap onto walls. Every lamp casts soft light across the room when it's on.

  • E on a light turns it on or off, with a gentle fade.
  • Ceiling fans ease up to speed over 3 seconds and coast down to a stop when you turn them off.
  • A light switch works every light and fan in its room. Several switches in one room all work together.
  • The Classic Steventechno fluorescent, square and wall lights are copied straight from the House Pack 3 model.
Towns & Cities → Living Room

Living room furniture

Sofas, chairs, tables, shelves, rugs and a fireplace, all drawn by the addon in several colours each. They snap square by square on a 32-unit grid.

  • Sit together: E sits you on the cushion you're looking at, so three people can share a three-seat sofa.
  • Sofas in Grey, Navy, Cream, Brown leather, Charcoal, Sage green, Mustard, Terracotta, Dusty pink and Black leather.
  • Up to eight dining chairs snap round the dining table.
  • The fireplace lights with E, flickers and lights up the room.
Towns & Cities → Decor

Decor and the little things

Plants, vases, frames, clocks, candles, books and more. The small ones go on any table, shelf, mantel or counter.

  • Clocks tell the real time: the wall clock, grandfather clock and mantel clock show your computer's clock.
  • Candles light with E and flicker. E again blows them out.
  • Wall outlets, thermostats and smoke detectors finish a room off.
Towns & Cities → Office

Office and gamer chairs

A Task chair, Office chair and Executive chair in white, grey or black, plus a Racing gamer chair and Gamer chair with footrest in ten colour schemes. They snap to desks and tables and seat you with E. Pair one with a desk and some Electronics Pack monitors for a full battlestation.

Towns & Cities → Outdoor

Outdoor and street pieces

Seventeen pieces for yards, streets and parking lots, each in several colours. Streetlamps light up with E and you can sit on the park bench. Many of them go straight onto sidewalks, no lot needed. See Street furniture.

Wall chip in every tab

Wall objects

Posters, paintings, mirrors, clocks, signs and wall TVs use the same hand. Press on a wall, move up or down to snap between Low, Middle, High and Top, slide sideways along the wall, then let go. Speakers from the Electronics Pack get extra heights from floor to ceiling.

Furnish sorts wall pieces out of the furniture packs automatically, so every pack's posters and paintings are ready to hang.

Towns & Cities → Wall

Curtains and pride flags

Curtains come in 23 colours: White, Cream, Beige, Grey, Charcoal, Black, Brown, Red, Burgundy, Orange, Mustard, Sage green, Forest green, Teal, Sky blue, Navy, Lavender, Purple and Pink, plus Rainbow and Pastel rainbow with horizontal or vertical stripes.

  • 1, 2 or 3 tiles wide, to match your windows.
  • Café, Sill, Long or Floor length.
  • E opens and closes them, in game or in build mode. The panels slide aside with deeper folds, and open or closed is saved.

Pride flags: 19 flags including Rainbow, Progress Pride, Intersex-Inclusive Progress, Transgender, Bisexual, Pansexual, Lesbian, Non-binary and more. Hang them horizontal or vertical (V turns it) in Small, Medium or Large.
